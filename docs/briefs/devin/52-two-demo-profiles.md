## Why

The team lead records the pitch video from the deployed site and needs two **generic, complete, obviously fake** example profiles, in two different zones, reachable by their own URLs, that behave the same on a machine that has old saved state in the browser. Today there is one demo (Mendoza, five parcels, `#demo`), its disclaimer is a small line, the tour dismissal and the saved fields live in `localStorage`, the live analysis takes 15 to 20 s, and the Mendoza farm cannot show rotations because vines and olives do not rotate. ## Build

1. **Two farms, two zones, exactly 3 parcels each (team lead correction: not 8).**
   - **Zone A, pampa húmeda (Buenos Aires province), 3 parcels, about 120 to 200 ha:** a fictional grain farm with three crops that form a recognizable rotation: `soja`, `maiz`, `trigo`. Pick a real rural location with visible field edges on the satellite base map (not the removed town; for example the Chacabuco, Junín or Pehuajó area), draw the boundary and the three parcels following real edges, and give each a plausible area and name. This is the profile that must end in the **four rotation cards**.
   - **Zone B, Cuyo (Mendoza), 3 parcels, about 80 to 150 ha:** the existing fictional farm in La Consulta (San Carlos) reduced to three parcels: `vid`, `olivo` and `ajo` (or `alfalfa` if `ajo` makes a poor comparison). Perennials do not rotate, so this profile shows fire, water, frost, heat, timeline, calendar and economics, and says in the Rotaciones tab why vines and olives are not rotated while the annual parcel does get a comparison if the engine supports it.
   - Each farm has a fictional producer profile: name, short story (two sentences), worries (hazard ranking: sequía, fuego, helada, El Niño, calor, ...), needs (water access, emergency distance, concerns), and illustrative economics per parcel (cost, price, yield). Names are generic ("Estancia La Aurora", "Finca Los Álamos"), no real people or companies.
2. **URLs (hash routes, in a registry `demoFarms.ts`):** `/app/#demo-pampa`, `/app/#demo-mendoza`; keep `/app/#demo` as an alias of the pampa farm (the landing links to it). Switching between them from a small "Otros ejemplos" control inside the demo works without reloading. Unknown demo hashes fall back to the pampa farm.
3. **"DEMO" in big letters, always visible.** A persistent, non-dismissible banner at the top center of the map while a demo is open: "DEMO · productor y finca ficticios" (EN: "DEMO · fictional producer and farm"), large (at least 20 px bold, high contrast on glass, 4.5:1 minimum over the brightest imagery), plus the same word as a corner ribbon so it survives any crop of the video; the existing disclaimer sentence stays in the result panel and explains what is real (location and satellite data) and what is invented (producer, names, economics). 4. **Clean state, independent of the browser.** In demo routes: ignore and never write `localStorage` saved fields ("Mis campos"), the saved hazard ranking and the economics, so the demo looks identical on a browser with old data; the guided tour **always starts** on a demo route (a "No volver a mostrar" is honored only inside the same session); `#demo-pampa&notour` skips the tour.
